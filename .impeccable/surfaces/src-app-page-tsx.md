# Surface: Landing (home)

## Scope and mode
- Route: `/` (`src/app/page.tsx` + `src/components/landing/*`)
- Visitor mode: **Persuade**
- Visual authority: inherit `DESIGN.md` (“Diagnóstico en Papel Blanco”) — no new world

## Audience and job
- Primary: person with a job (or existing business) evaluating **one** idea under current constraints
- Job: believe Decida judges *their* idea with grounded criteria, then start `/analizar`
- Primary action: Coral CTA → `/analizar` ($99 MXN)
- Secondary: quiet path → `/ejemplo`

## Proof and product truth
- Allowed proof: report preview/example, analysis dimensions, honest fit/non-fit, FAQ
- Never invent testimonials or social proof
- Unique claim: evaluates the idea they already have against their constraints — does not pick a business for them

## Chosen direction: Constraint Mirror
- Hero and flow mirror the user’s constraints (time, money, risk, goals) as the diagnostic lens
- Sequence:
  1. Hero — one decision question + constraint subtitle + coral CTA + quiet ejemplo link; **one** proof artifact (not noisy carousel)
  2. Problem — ideas fail when they hit real conditions (tighter, less repetitive copy)
  3. Mechanism — merge “qué analiza” + “cómo funciona” into one clear band
  4. Proof — sample report judgment (more serious / founded)
  5. Fit — para ti / no para ti
  6. Price — after proof; “beta” as quiet footnote, not loud badge
  7. FAQ — remaining objections
  8. Final CTA — repeat primary action
- Focal moment: hero → decide to analyze
- Motion: soft micro-transitions only (Quiet Motion) — fluid, not spectacle
- Flat: no decorative shadows/gradients that fight DESIGN.md

## Boundaries
- Untouched: product claims, $99 MXN, routes, DESIGN tokens
- Anti-goals: motivational-coach tone, vague/repetitive UX writing, loud beta chrome, fintech/SaaS/purple, shadow/glow theater, abrupt motion

## Open for build (must not invent)
- Exact hero/mechanism copy (needs editorial pass aligned to PRODUCT voice)
- Whether hero proof is a static fragment or a crop of `/ejemplo`
- Exact motion intensity (minimum useful)
